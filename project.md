🐟 Pixel Fishing – One More Cast!

A tiny, addictive pixel-art fishing game where the goal is simple: catch fish, earn money, upgrade your gear, and become the richest fisherman in the ocean.

But the ocean has a weird sense of humor. 😂
🎮 Gameplay
- Cast your fishing rod and wait for a bite.
- Catch fish of different rarities.
- Sell your catch at the fish market.
- Upgrade your rod, fishing line, boat, and bait.
- Unlock new fishing locations.
- Discover rare and legendary sea creatures.
😂 Funny elements
- Catch an old shoe instead of a fish.
- Sometimes a fish steals your bait.
- Occasionally catch a message in a bottle saying "Help, I'm a developer."
- Catch a giant fish that pulls your boat.
- Find a legendary golden toilet seat.
🪙 Progression
Upgrade	Benefit
Better Rod	Catch bigger fish
Stronger Line	Catch heavy fish
Better Bait	Attract rare fish
Bigger Boat	Explore deeper waters
Fish Finder	Reveal nearby rare fish

🎨 Visual style
- Retro 2D pixel art.
- Small animated fisherman.
- Blue ocean with moving waves.
- Cute fish animations.
- Relaxing background music.
- Funny sound effects.
The addictive loop
Cast → Catch → Sell → Upgrade → Explore → Repeat.

The secret sauce: make every fishing trip feel like there's a chance of discovering something ridiculous.
